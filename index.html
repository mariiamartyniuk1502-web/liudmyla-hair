Personalised Pricing

Every hair transformation is unique, and so is the price. All prices are calculated individually.

Your personalised quote is based on:

  • Hair length
  • Hair thickness
  • The colouring technique chosen
  • The complexity of the service
  • The amount of time and professional products required

To receive an accurate quotation, please send a few recent photos of your hair when making your booking enquiry.

Please note: All prices are discussed and confirmed individually before your appointment.
